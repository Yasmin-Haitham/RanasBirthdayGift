# 🌻 Happy Birthday, Rana

A personal birthday website built with love by miso.

## What this is

A single-page interactive birthday experience for Rana's 23rd birthday — celebrating April 11th, fashionably late.

The site includes:

- An animated Van Gogh Starry Night hero with live canvas stars and swirling sky
- A full guide to the works featured in the **Beyond Van Gogh: The Immersive Experience** exhibition at District 5, New Cairo — with fun science facts and reflection prompts for each painting
- A "what are you craving?" quiz that tallies answers and reveals the dinner destination (Antakha or Mo Bistro)
- A full adventure map of the day's stops
- Two sealed letters that open on tap — to be read at the right moment

## How to use

Just open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no dependencies, no internet required after the page loads (except for Google Fonts).
